Compliance & Banking Disclosures

Dream Hatcher Tech operates as a digital technology and software solutions provider in full alignment with Nigerian regulatory standards.

Financial Services & Settlement Model

Dream Hatcher Tech does not hold itself out as a direct deposit-taking bank. All monetary wallet operations, dedicated virtual accounts, and customer funds are processed exclusively through licensed commercial banks and Payment Service Providers (PSPs) regulated by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN).

Banking Rails

Virtual accounts provisioned via CBN-licensed banks (Wema / Providus Bank).

Payment Switching

Payment webhooks and settlement via licensed PSPs (Monnify / Squad Co).

NCC Aggregators

Direct connectivity to NCC-licensed telecom aggregators.

Identity Verification

Automated verification pipelines integrated with regulated identity providers.

KYC and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Policy

In compliance with the Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act and Central Bank of Nigeria guidelines, Dream Hatcher Tech implements strict Know-Your-Customer (KYC) procedures across all digital products.

Customer data is handled in strict compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA).
